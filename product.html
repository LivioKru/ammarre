Care

Salt water does the line no harm — it is what the rope was built for. Rinse in fresh water and let it dry off the wrist. The steel will dull evenly with wear; leave it. If you want it back, a grey kitchen pad along the grain of the brush.

Keep it off sunscreen and perfume, which stain the cover before they touch the steel.

Shipping and returns
