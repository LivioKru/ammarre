FAQ

The name

Ammarrare — to moor. To bring a boat alongside and make it fast: a hold that is deliberate, worked out in advance, and temporary by design. You tie up in order to leave again.

Fit & sizing

Measure the wrist, not a bracelet you already own. Run a tape where a watch sits, snug but not tight — two turns take a little over twice that length. Add a piece to the bag and choose your size there.

Material & construction

5 mm braided yacht line, a braided cover over a load-bearing core, closed with a brushed 316L stainless shackle — a screw pin, the same fitting that holds a boat to a cleat. Ends are whipped and set into steel so the braid cannot open.

Shipping & care

Sent from Zürich — Switzerland in two working days, Europe in three to five. Salt water does the line no harm; rinse in fresh water and let it dry off the wrist. The steel dulls evenly with wear — leave it, or bring it back with a grey kitchen pad along the grain of the brush.
